Prompt: Explain the difference between stack and queue in simple terms.

MODEL RESPONSE:

A stack is like a stack of plates. You can only add or remove plates from the top of the stack. You can only add or remove plates from the bottom of the stack. The order of the plates is preserved, and the last plate you added is the first plate you remove.

A queue is like a line of people waiting to get a drink. You can only add people to the end of the line, and you can only serve people from the front of the line. The order of the people in the line is preserved, and the first person in line is the first person to be served.

In programming, stacks are often used to implement functions that need to be called in a specific order, such as a function that needs to print a series of numbers in reverse order. Queues are often used to implement functions that need to wait for input or to process input in a specific order, such as a function that needs to process a series of emails in order of arrival.
