QUESTION 1: Laptop A costs Rs. 60,000 and has a 10% discount. What is the final price?

--- WITHOUT CoT ---
The discount amount is  

\[
10\% \times 60{,}000 = 0.10 \times 60{,}000 = 6{,}000 \text{ rupees}
\]

Subtracting the discount from the original price:

\[
60{,}000 - 6{,}000 = 54{,}000 \text{ rupees}
\]

**Final price:** **Rs. 54,000**.

--- WITH REASONING ---
**Step‑by‑step calculation**

1. **Original price** of Laptop A = Rs 60,000.  

2. **Discount rate** = 10 %.  

3. **Amount of discount**  
   \[
   \text{Discount} = \frac{10}{100}\times 60{,}000
                  = 0.10 \times 60{,}000
                  = 6{,}000\ \text{Rs}
   \]

4. **Final price** after subtracting the discount  
   \[
   \text{Final price} = \text{Original price} - \text{Discount}
                      = 60{,}000 - 6{,}000
                      = 54{,}000\ \text{Rs}
   \]

---

**Answer:** The final price of Laptop A after a 10 % discount is **Rs 54,000**.

\[
